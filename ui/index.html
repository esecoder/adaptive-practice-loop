Adaptive Practice Loop

What this is. A working slice of an exam-prep platform: it explains why an answer was wrong, refuses to explain when the approved content does not cover the question, and picks what to study next. Every number on this page comes from the API running behind it — nothing here is mocked up.

What to do — about 60 seconds. The three panels below run in order: try to make it refuse (§1), send a write five times (§2), then ask what to practise next (§3). Each one shows you something a README cannot.

0. Bring your own content

⚠️ You do not have to test with my syllabus. Paste any document below and every panel underneath answers from your text instead. That is the point: the tutor decides from how much of your question the corpus covers, so the honest demonstration is to hand it a corpus it has never seen.

Headings of the form ## id — Title start a section; otherwise blank lines separate passages. Which one ran is reported back.

1. The tutor will refuse

⚠️ Answers come from the corpus loaded in §0 — the bundled syllabus until you replace it. Type something it does not cover. The tutor abstains rather than inventing an explanation, and the question is routed to a human as a content gap. Abstention is a product decision, not an error — a tutor that cannot abstain eventually tells a candidate they misunderstood something they did not, which is the one failure an educational product cannot ship.
